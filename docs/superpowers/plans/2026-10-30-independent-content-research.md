# Independent Official Research

Approved: remove Gemini/Google research dependency from content generation.

- Match the public theme to normalized metadata from registered government sources.
- If no stored match, collect current Receita Federal, Fazenda and eSocial listings directly with existing HTTPS, time and memory limits.
- Never send the private context or credentials to publishers.
- Provide only short excerpts, URLs and publication dates to the writer; these are partial references, not legal validation.
- Gemini and OpenAI receive no search tools. Gemini now makes only one structured drafting request.
- Retain original news attribution separately and show a mandatory review warning when no official evidence is available.
- Enable independent research for both providers, preserve the user's checkbox preference across provider changes, allow enough client timeout for collection plus drafting.

Coverage is the stored government archive and selected recent official listings, not a whole-web or comprehensive legal database. Writing still depends on the configured AI account's generation quota.
